This website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse traffic, and personalise content.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and improve our services.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the basic functioning of the site (e.g., session management, CSRF protection).
  • Analytics Cookies: We run our own privacy-friendly visit counter on this site. It stores one random, anonymous ID (anid) in a cookie for up to 1 year so we can see how many people visit, which pages are useful, and which device types to support. It never records your name, contact details, invoices, or anything typed into the site, and it is not linked to your account.
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your settings and preferences.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
