Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: {% now "d M Y" %}

This policy explains how returns, replacements and refunds are handled by {{ company.name }}{% if company.city %}, {{ company.city|default:company.state }}{% endif %}.

1. Products Purchased In-Store / Online

  • Physical products (accessories, spares, systems) may be returned within 7 days of purchase.
  • The product must be unused, undamaged, complete with all accessories, packing and the original bill.
  • Products sold as "refurbished" or "as-is / spotted" are not eligible for return unless a defect is found within 48 hours of purchase.
  • Software, licences, AMCs and services once activated/delivered are not returnable.

2. Manufacturing Defects

Defective products are replaced or repaired under the manufacturer's warranty. We pass on the full manufacturer warranty available in India and help you with the claim process. If a replacement piece is unavailable, a refund of the product value (excluding labour and service charges) will be issued.

3. Repairs

  • If the reported fault reappears within the warranty period printed on your job card, we recheck and repair it free of charge.
  • If we are unable to fix a device, no repair charge is taken; only diagnosis charges (if informed and approved in advance) apply.
  • Wrong or unnecessary part replacement found on our side will be corrected and refunded.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

  • Returns must be initiated by WhatsApp/call/visit with your bill number.
  • After inspection and approval, refunds are processed within 5–7 working days.
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method (UPI/bank) or paid in cash against the bill, wherever applicable.
  • Advance payments for jobs that are cancelled before work starts are refunded in full.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • Labour / service charges for work already completed.
  • Data recovery attempts (charged for effort; success is never guaranteed).
  • Products damaged by liquid ingress, physical damage, over-voltage or unauthorised repair attempts.

6. Contact

For any return or refund request, reach us at {{ company.phone }} / WhatsApp or {{ company.email }}. Related pages: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.
