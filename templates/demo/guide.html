🛠 Computer Repair & Accounting Software — Demo Guide

Hindi-English Tutorial · Poora professional system · 6-month evaluation

🚀 1. Software kaise chalayein (How to start)

  • Desktop icon "Computer Repair Demo" par double-click karein. Program apne aap local server start karega aur browser khul jayega.
  • Browser me address: http://127.0.0.1:8777/
  • Band karne ke liye browser tab band karein; server background me band ho jata hai (Task Manager se "Computer Repair Demo" process end kar sakte hain).
  • Aapka demo data save hota hai: %LOCALAPPDATA%\A1RepairDemo\ — uninstall ke baad bhi rehta hai, delete karne par fresh demo ban jata hai. Sab data sirf aapke computer par — kahin internet par nahi jata.

📋 3. Repair Workflow — sabse important feature

  1. Repairs → New Repair: customer ka device, problem, warranty entry. Job number auto-banta hai (REP-0001…).
  2. Status board: Pending → Received → Under Diagnosis → Under Repair → Ready → Delivered (ya Cancel).
  3. Estimate banayein (parts + labour add karke) phir "Send Estimate" — poora professional WhatsApp message ready hota hai (itemised breakup, total, approve/reject link).
  4. Customer approve kare ya aap staff ki taraf se confirm karke seedha Invoice generate karein.
  5. Tracking link bina-login ke chalta hai — customer phone par status dekh sakta hai.

Chip-level repair dukaan ke liye poora job-card system — entry se delivery tak, GST invoice ke saath.

🧾 4. Billing & GST (Sales)

  • Sales → New Invoice: items search/add, discount, GST (B2B/Non-GST/Inter-state/Intra-state CGST+SGST) auto.
  • Invoice print/PDF professional format me — company naam, GSTIN, bill number sab Settings se aate hain.
  • Invoice par WhatsApp share button — customer ko summary chala jata hai.
  • Credit Notes (return), invoice se payment receive, pending/paid filters.

📦 5. Purchases & Stock

  • Purchases: vendor bill entry → stock automatically badhta hai, payment settlement (full/partial/advance).
  • Stock dashboard: low-stock alerts, stock adjustment (damage/sample), value report.
  • Products: parts + services (labour), barcode/HSN, selling price.

💰 6. Payments, Bank & Cash Book

  • Payments: received/paid, cash-bank-UPI, advance handling, expense heads (Rent, Salary…).
  • Cash Book + Bank Accounts: day-wise cash, bank statement print, contra transfers.

📊 7. Accounting Reports (Tally-style)

  • Journals auto-post: har invoice/payment/expense se ledger update.
  • Reports: Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Customer/Vendor Ledger, Aging (kab se pending), GST report — sab print/PDF/Excel.

⚡ 8. Speed features

  • Keyboard shortcuts (Tally style): page par ? dabayein — poora key list.
  • Global search (/ key): customer, invoice, product, job — sab ek jagah.
  • Notifications: stock kam, payment due, repair updates — bell icon me + email bhi.
  • Visitors/Analytics: kaun customer kab aaya, WhatsApp link clicks.
  • Database Health: backup, safe cleanup, storage status.

🎨 9. Apni company kaise lagayein (Dynamic branding)

Staff login → Settings — sirf yahan apna naam, logo, address, phone, GSTIN, city, website bharein. Poori site, bills, WhatsApp messages, emails, reports — sab turant aapke brand ho jayenge. Koi code change nahi, kori setting.

Software 100% aapki company ke liye customize hone wala hai — demo ka naam sirf example hai.
