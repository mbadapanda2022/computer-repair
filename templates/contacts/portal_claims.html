Portal Linking Requests

People who signed up on the website and look like an existing contact (same email or mobile). Nothing is linked automatically — approve the right record, or reject to give them a brand-new profile.

Back to Contacts
How to decide
Approve when the signup is the same person as the suggested record — they immediately see that record's older invoices, repairs and payments in the portal. The old record keeps everything; only empty fields (email, phone) are filled. Reject when it is a different person (for example a recycled phone number) — the customer gets a separate, empty profile.
