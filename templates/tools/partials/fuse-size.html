{# Fuse sizing — survive the load, still protect the wiring #}

Fuse Size

A fuse sits between two limits: it must not open while the board is working, and it must open before the track or cable it guards cooks. The blow character decides how much slack it needs, because a switching supply and a motor both wake up pulling far more than they run on.

Cartridge to fit
—
Ratings are snapped up to the standard IEC cartridge series (…1, 1.25, 1.6, 2, 2.5, 3.15, 4, 5, 6.3 A…) and every rating is quoted at 25 °C, so a fuse strapped to a hot heatsink carries less than its stamp. On mains work use a 250 V cartridge, and never fit anything above the ampacity of the wiring behind it.
