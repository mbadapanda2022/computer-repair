{# PCB trace current capacity (IPC-2221) #}

PCB Trace Current

Before you jumper a burnt track or run a wire where a trace used to be: how much current a copper track may carry without cooking its own adhesive, and how wide it has to be for the rail you are repairing.

IPC-2221 empirical relation, for a trace on a plain FR-4 board in still air. An inner layer carries roughly half of an outer one because it is buried in prepreg. Soldered-up traces, heavy copper pours and forced airflow all do better than these numbers, so treat them as the floor.
