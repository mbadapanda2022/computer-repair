Rectifier & Bulk Cap

A bridge and a capacitor give a rail that is never flat — the cap only refills twice per cycle, so the rail sags in between. Enter the load and the capacitance to see the voltage you will actually read, and how much ripple is riding on it.

This is the linear supply maths: silicon diodes are about 0.7 V each, Schottky about 0.4 V, and 50 Hz is the Indian mains (60 Hz elsewhere). A switching supply's output cap is sized by the ripple current its ESR can take, not by this formula — but a dried-out electrolyte shows up here as exactly the sag a customer complains about.
