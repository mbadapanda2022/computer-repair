{# Linear regulator drop and heat — what an LDO or 78xx has to shed #}

Regulator Drop & Heat

A linear regulator spends every volt it removes as heat, so a hot 5 V chip tells you two things at once: how much drop it is eating and how much current is pulling it. Work the limit backwards and you also get the current where it stops being safe.

Running temperature
—
θJA off a datasheet is usually quoted still-air on a small test board: a TO-220 with no sink is about 62 °C/W, a SOT-223 on a plain board about 95 °C/W, and the same part over a solid copper pour can drop under 40. If a rail reads correct but the chip is burning hot, look for the shorted load drawing the extra current before replacing the regulator.
