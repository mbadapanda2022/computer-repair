{# Current sense resistor — millivolts per amp and the wattage to fit #}

Current Sense Resistor

The low-value leg in a charger, a battery protection board or a laptop rail is just a known resistor: the millivolts across it are the current. Work it forwards to size a replacement, or backwards from what your meter is reading to find the current flowing right now.

Signal per amp
—
When a sense resistor has burnt open, the rail downstream reads dead even though the supply is fine — measure across it rather than trusting the band or code, because a darkened 0.01 Ω part often still measures right while cold. Replace it with the same value, not a nearby one: the whole current loop is calibrated against those millivolts.
