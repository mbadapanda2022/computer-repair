{# Voltage divider — unloaded tap and the real loaded output #}

Voltage Divider

Two resistors make the tap voltage, but the circuit on the tap makes it something else. Enter the load to see the sag you will actually measure with a meter, and whether the divider is stiff enough to trust.

On the tap
—
A divider feeding an ADC input, a sense line or a transistor base is only accurate while the bottom leg is at least a hundred times smaller than what hangs on it. Otherwise buffer the tap with an op-amp, or pick lower resistor values and accept the wasted current.
