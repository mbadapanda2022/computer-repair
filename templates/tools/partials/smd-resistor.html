{# SMD chip resistor marking decoder #}

SMD Code → Resistance

Type what is printed on the chip. The last digit is the number of zeros, and R marks the decimal point.

Try:
Resistance
—
A blank (no printing at all) is usually a 0 Ω jumper or a measurement-pad link, not an unmarked resistor. EIA-96 letter codes such as 68C are not decoded here — different published tables disagree, so we would rather show nothing than a wrong value. Check those against the manufacturer's table.
