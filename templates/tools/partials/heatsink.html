{# Heatsink sizing and junction temperature #}

Heatsink & Junction Temp

Heat falls down the same way voltage does: junction to case, case to sink, sink to air, each one a resistance. Fill the path in and read the junction temperature — or leave the sink blank and get the biggest heatsink that still keeps the part legal.

Running temperature
—
Typical numbers: a TO-220 is about 3–5 °C/W junction-to-case and roughly 50–62 °C/W to still air on its own, so anything above about 1.5 W wants a sink. A mica or silicone pad costs 0.5–1 °C/W, grease less. Design for 100 °C, not 150 °C — the electrolytics next to it age twice as fast for every 10 °C.
