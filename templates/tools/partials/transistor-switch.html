{# Transistor switch base resistor (saturation) #}

Transistor Base Resistor

A driver transistor on a printer motor board or a laptop enable line is used as a switch, so it is pushed into saturation instead of being run at its datasheet gain. Enter the load and the drive voltage and get the resistor that actually turns it on hard.

Base resistor
—
Overdrive 2 is normal, 5 to 10 is what most service manuals use for a switching transistor, and hFE on the datasheet is quoted at a different collector current than your load — so drive it hard rather than trusting the gain. For a MOSFET there is no DC gate current: use 10–100 Ω in the gate lead to tame the switching spike instead.
