{# RC time constant — charge, discharge, inrush and stored energy #}

RC Time Constant

The same τ answers two bench questions: how long a soft-start, reset line or timer takes to reach its level, and how long a bulk capacitor stays charged after the board is unplugged.

Time constant
—
Charging follows V = Vs(1 − e−t/τ) and discharging V = Vs·e−t/τ, with τ = R × C. Five time constants is 99.3 %, which is why a board is not safe to touch the moment the rail reads zero.
